John's Pork began in 1967 with John Seidler, a passionate cook whose love for creating memorable dishes grew from his family's pig farm. What started as a dream to bring quality pork dishes to the table soon became a family tradition. Built on hard work, dedication, and a passion for great food, John's Pork has grown into a restaurant that celebrates the art of fine dining.

For nearly 60 years, the Seidler family has carried on John's legacy, passing down recipes, techniques, and a commitment to excellence through generations. Each generation has brought its own ideas and creativity while staying true to the values that made the restaurant special. Today, John's Pork remains a family-driven establishment where tradition and innovation come together in every dish.

At John's Pork, we believe that an exceptional dining experience goes beyond the food itself. From the careful selection of ingredients to the elegant presentation of each plate, every detail is thoughtfully considered. Our menu highlights the rich flavors and versatility of pork while also offering other carefully crafted dishes to satisfy a variety of tastes. We strive to create meals that are as memorable as the moments shared around the table.

Whether you are joining us for a special occasion, a business dinner, or simply an evening of fine dining, our goal is to make every visit feel extraordinary. We take pride in providing a welcoming atmosphere, attentive service, and food prepared with care and passion. At John's Pork, we invite you to become part of our story and experience a family tradition that has been bringing people together since 1967.
